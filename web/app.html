Attest calls the Settlement Recon API directly. Your key is used for the length of one request and then discarded — it is never written to the database, to a log, or into any prompt.

Test keys only. A live Razorpay secret can create refunds and move real money, so this app refuses one. Razorpay can give you your settlements — it cannot give you your bank statement, so without one your proof rate will be zero however well the totals tie. That is not a bug: Razorpay cannot prove Razorpay.
